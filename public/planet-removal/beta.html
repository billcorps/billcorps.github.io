BEFORE YOU START

One account.
All three steps.

Use the Google account you use in the Play Store on your Android device. Choose that same account in Google Groups and on the testing page.

  • Android 8.0 or newer
  • A Google account and Google Play
  • Free to test · no purchase needed

DURING THE TEST

Make a few removals.
Tell us how it goes.

Pull back and release the launcher. Remove the gold targets, protect the cyan client, and try to keep the star stable.

Try pausing and resuming, changing the sound or effects settings, and playing again after a loss. Stay opted in while you test to receive new beta builds through Google Play.

GETTING STARTED

Having trouble
joining?

Most joining problems come down to the selected account or a step that’s still waiting.

Google Play says the test isn’t available

Check that you’re a confirmed member of the tester group, then open the testing link with that same Google account. Check the account selected in the Android Play Store, too. New tests and updates can take several hours to become available. If it still won’t open, ask in the tester group ↗.

My group membership is awaiting approval

A pending request is not confirmed membership. Wait for your request to be approved, then return to step 2 to opt into the Google Play test.

I’m already an internal tester

Leave the internal test first, then use the closed-test link in step 2 to opt into this beta. Google Play doesn’t let an internal tester receive a closed test at the same time.

Can I play in the browser instead?

Yes—play the browser edition with touch or a mouse. Browser play doesn’t enroll you in the Android test. Follow the three steps above to test the Android app. The two editions keep separate saves.
