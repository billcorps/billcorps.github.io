Policy.

How Planet Removal handles progress, privacy, and online services.

UPDATED OCTOBER 9, 2026 · PLANET REMOVAL / BILLCORPS

The game saves progress on your device. The browser edition has no advertising, analytics, or account sign-in. The Android app uses Google services for advertising and its optional global leaderboard, and may participate in Android system backups.

Privacy policy

The full privacy policy covers the Android app, browser game, advertising service data, Google Play Games, and GitHub hosting. It explains which information stays local and how to manage service data.

Local saves and backups

Progress and preferences are saved locally. The Android app and browser edition have separate saves. The game provides no billcorps or Play Games cloud-save service; Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device and account backup settings. See Android's backup documentation. The leaderboard is not a backup of your current run.

In the browser game, Settings lets you download a save backup or restore one. Keep a backup if you want to preserve progress before clearing site data. Starting a new run replaces the current run while keeping preferences and lifetime statistics.

Clearing this site's browser data removes browser saves and settings, but does not remove backup files you have downloaded. Clearing Planet Removal's storage in Android settings removes the app's local progress and preferences. Clearing local storage does not delete records held separately by Google services or Android backups. If browser storage is blocked or full, gameplay can continue in memory, but progress may not survive closing or reloading the page.

Advertising choices

The browser edition does not display ads. In the Android app, native ads can appear in the menu, pause, replay, and leaderboard screens. Choosing Play again after losing has a one-in-ten chance of an interstitial when one is available.

Where required, the Android app presents Google's consent message before requesting ads. Privacy options allow you to revisit your choices when Google makes that option available. See the privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for the SDK's service data.

One global leaderboard

The Android integration is designed for one global, all-time Google Play Games leaderboard. Participation is optional and requires an internet connection and available, configured Play Games services. You can play without signing in.

Leaderboard availability is shown in the game. The browser edition currently does not submit scores or display player rankings. No separate browser leaderboard is maintained.

Website hosting

The site and browser game load their files from GitHub Pages. The site adds no tracking cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address, while serving those files; its privacy statement explains its handling of that information.

Questions and updates

For help, visit Support. For privacy questions, use the contact information on the billcorps GitHub profile. Avoid posting passwords, account identifiers, or private save files in public.

These pages are updated when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies this policy overview; the full privacy policy has its own effective date.
