01

Pull. Release. Commit.

Pull back from the launcher and release. A longer pull adds power. Planets keep orbiting while you aim, and gravity bends your fireball in flight.

TIMING + GRAVITY
02

Finish the removal.

Chip away at the gold targets. Expose and hit a planet’s core, or leave 35% of its material or less. Remove every requested planet to complete the contract.

DESTRUCTIBLE WORLDS
03

Keep the client safe.

The cyan planet must stay untouched. Any client damage ends the job. Keep the central star stable, too—a careless shot can turn a contract into a catastrophe.
